#inglese 

Questo metodo di insegnamento consiste nel far dire agli studenti una parola. 

**Esempio: Elicit "Treat"**:
- "Look at the picture: is he old or young?" - "Old"
- "And who do you think the girl is?" - "His daughter"
- "Do you think his daughter has a child?" - "Yes"
- "What do you think you can give to the child?" - "Toys, sweets"
- "What's the word used when we give something as a reward" - ???
- "It's called 'treat'. Now repeat 'treat'" - "Treat"
